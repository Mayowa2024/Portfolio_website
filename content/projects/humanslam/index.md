HumanSLAM explores a human-inspired approach to visual place recognition using scene context, stable objects, spatial layout, and visible text to support geometric SLAM.

## Focus

- Semantic place recognition under appearance change
- Scene, object-spatial, and textual reasoning
- Integration with ORB-SLAM3
- Real-time evaluation across robotics datasets
